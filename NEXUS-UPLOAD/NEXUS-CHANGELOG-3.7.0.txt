[size=4]3.7.0[/size]

[b]Your follower can turn into a creature now.[/b] Dragons, wolves, trolls, spriggans, whatever
your mods provide. Combat transformation had been quietly hiding every creature race, which
left it offering only the same people-races she could already be. On a big load order that is
191 races before and 944 after.

[b]Choosing an outfit and armour no longer breaks the build.[/b] If you gave her a legacy outfit
and also picked armour pieces, the build stopped with "Must be fixed" and one error per piece.
That combination was always meant to work: the outfit decides what she starts in, and the
pieces sit in her inventory so you can trade them to her. It now says that instead of failing.

[b]The build tells you when a RaceMenu preset's body shape won't come across.[/b] A plugin can
store a face, not a body. If your preset carries body sliders you get a warning pointing you at
BodySlide or OBody, where that shape actually belongs. Overlays - tattoos, warpaint, scars -
don't transfer either, and it says so.

Smaller things:
[list]
[*]Creature races show their FormID and EditorID, because several mods ship a race called
"BabyDragonRace" and you need to know which one you picked.
[*]One clear warning about the outfit/armour combination instead of two vague ones.
[/list]

[size=4]Also included, if you are coming from 3.6.0[/size]

3.6.1 never made it to Nexus, so it ships here:

[list]
[*][b]You can unselect things.[/b] Every optional picker has a Clear button, and the Expert
Deck has Clear selection. Previously nothing could be removed once picked, and the checkmark
column looked clickable but never was - it is a status light, and now says so.
[*][b]Copy diagnostics[/b] on the Review page (also Ctrl+K). One click copies everything I need
to help with a bug report. Your Windows username is stripped out, so it is safe to paste in a
public comment.
[*]Window no longer clips its right edge on smaller screens, and the build buttons wrap instead
of hiding off the edge.
[/list]

[size=4]Worth knowing[/size]

[list]
[*]Transformation is EXPERIMENTAL and adds a script. Scripts stay in save files.
[*]A beast form cannot wear or wield her gear. That is Skyrim's behaviour; reverting brings it
back.
[*]Creature races have no face to customise, and their dialogue depends entirely on the mod
that added them.
[/list]

FollowerForge is a Windows program, not a Skyrim mod - do not install it into Data, Vortex or
MO2 as game content. Extract it anywhere else and run FollowerForge.exe.
