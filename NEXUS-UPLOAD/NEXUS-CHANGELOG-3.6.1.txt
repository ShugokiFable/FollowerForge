FollowerForge 3.6.1

New
- "Copy diagnostics" button on the Review page (also in the Ctrl+K palette). One click puts a
  short plain-text report on your clipboard: which mod manager and profile you use, how many
  plugins were indexed, what you picked for the follower, and what the last build reported.
  If something goes wrong, paste that into your bug report or comment and it can usually be
  answered without a back-and-forth first.
- Your Windows account name is not in it. Home folders are written as %USERPROFILE% and
  %LOCALAPPDATA%, so the report is safe to paste in a public comment.

Housekeeping
- Build/CI cleanup and corrected release notes. No change to how followers are built.

Known, and not fixed in this version
- RaceMenu OVERLAYS (tattoos, warpaint, body paint) still do not carry over to a built
  follower. Sculpt, tint and head shape do. A warning for this is the next thing on the list.

Upgrading: unzip over your existing copy, or into a new folder. Your settings, catalogue and
saved followers are untouched.
