FollowerForge 3.2.8 — MO2 masters fix

What was broken
- After 3.2.7 could browse ModOrganizer.ini and pick the right profile, builds still
  looked for plugin masters (e.g. SOSVoices.esm) only under the Steam game Data folder.
- MO2 never puts those files in Steam Data; they live in enabled mod folders.
- Error looked like:
  Build failed: Required master 'SOSVoices.esm' is not installed in
  '...\Skyrim Special Edition\Data'.

What 3.2.8 fixes
- Before writing the follower ESP, FollowerForge builds its MO2 hardlink plugin view
  and expands the master chain from that view (same place catalogue indexing already uses).
- Master presence checks, combat-style / cell open, CharGen export lookup, face textures,
  and own-hub asset copies also resolve through MO2 mods when needed.
- Vortex users: no behavior change.

How to update
1. Close FollowerForge.
2. Replace the previous folder with this release (or overwrite the two EXEs).
3. Open FollowerForge, use MO2 setup if needed, re-index if prompted, build again.

Your MO2 instance, mods, profiles, and Skyrim Data are never modified by FollowerForge.
