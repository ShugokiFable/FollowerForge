FollowerForge 3.7.0 — she can turn into a dragon now, and the outfit bug is dead

[size=4]Creature forms in combat transformation[/size]

"Custom race / spell" quietly refused to show you a single creature race. Every dragon, wolf,
troll, spriggan and beast form your mods provide was filtered out, which left the feature
offering the same people-races your follower could already be.

The filter was right in the wrong place. Creature races are hidden from the [b]identity[/b]
picker because they carry no head data, so no face can be built for one. That is a rule about
the race she IS. It says nothing about the race she TURNS INTO — her face belongs to her base
race, and the transformation only swaps races once a fight has started. Vanilla werewolf, which
this feature already offered, is a creature race itself.

On a 2,920-plugin load order that is 191 races before and 944 after. Creature rows now show
their FormID and EditorID too, because five installed mods can each ship something called
"BabyDragonRace" and you need to know which one you are picking.

[size=4]"Must be fixed" when you chose an outfit and armour[/size]

Reported by a user: they put clothing in the follower's inventory, gave her a legacy outfit,
and the build stopped with errors and no way back — because 3.6.0 had no way to unselect
anything either. 3.6.1 fixed the unselecting. This fixes the error itself.

Armour plus a legacy outfit was a guaranteed hard failure, one error per piece. The combination
has always been supported: the outfit decides what she starts in, and the pieces stay in her
inventory so you can trade them to her. Only the checker disagreed — it looked for your
Skyrim.esm outfit inside the plugin FollowerForge had just written, did not find it, and called
every piece missing.

It now says so instead, once, naming the outfit: she starts in that, the pieces are in her pack,
clear the outfit if you want her wearing your own picks.

[size=4]The body shape that never came across[/size]

A RaceMenu preset can carry a whole body in its bodyMorphs block — OBody and BodySlide write
their sliders there. A plugin has nowhere to store that: an NPC record holds head data, not a
body. So it has always been dropped, silently.

The build now counts the sliders you actually shaped and tells you, and points at BodySlide or
OBody as the place that shape belongs. It names RaceMenu overlays in the same breath — tattoos,
warpaint and scars applied as overlay layers do not transfer either.

[size=4]Also[/size]

[list]
[*]LEGACY_OUTFIT_WITH_EQUIPMENT is gone. It said the engine "may choose" the outfit over your
gear. It does choose it — there was never any doubt, and two warnings for one situation is noise.
[*]FollowerForge.Cli.exe races --creatures lists what the transformation picker offers.
[/list]

489 automated tests pass. The exact reported combination — legacy outfit, two armour pieces and
a creature transform race — builds end to end with one warning and no errors.

[size=4]Still true, and still worth knowing[/size]

[list]
[*]Transformation is EXPERIMENTAL and adds a script. Scripts persist in save files.
[*]A beast form cannot wear or wield her equipment. That is Skyrim's behaviour; reverting
restores it.
[*]Creature races have no face to customise and their dialogue depends entirely on the mod
that added them.
[/list]
