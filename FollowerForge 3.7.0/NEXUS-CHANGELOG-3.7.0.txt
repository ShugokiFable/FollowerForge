3.7.0

- Combat transformation can now turn her into a creature. Dragons, wolves, trolls, spriggans,
  whatever your mods provide. Every creature race was being hidden from that picker, so it only
  ever offered the same people-races she could already be.

- Picking an outfit AND armour no longer breaks the build. That combination used to stop with
  "Must be fixed" and one error per piece. The outfit decides what she starts in, the armour
  sits in her inventory so you can trade it to her, and the build now just says so.

- New warning when a RaceMenu preset carries a body shape. A plugin can store a face, not a
  body, so that shape has always been dropped. Use a BodySlide or OBody preset for it instead.
  Overlays - tattoos, warpaint, scars - do not transfer either, and it now tells you.

- Creature races show their FormID and EditorID, so you can tell apart the several mods that
  all ship a race called "BabyDragonRace".

Also included, if you are coming from 3.6.0 (3.6.1 never went up on Nexus)

- You can unselect things. Every optional picker has a Clear button and the Expert Deck has
  Clear selection. Before this, nothing could be removed once picked.

- Copy diagnostics on the Review page (or Ctrl+K). One click copies what I need to help with a
  bug report. Your Windows username is stripped out, so it is safe to paste publicly.

- The window no longer clips its right edge on smaller screens, and the build buttons wrap
  instead of hiding off the edge.

Worth knowing

- Transformation is EXPERIMENTAL and adds a script. Scripts stay in save files.
- A beast form cannot wear or wield her gear. That is Skyrim's behaviour; reverting brings it
  back.
- Creature races have no face to customise, and their dialogue depends on the mod that added
  them.
- FollowerForge is a Windows program, not a Skyrim mod. Do not install it into Data, Vortex or
  MO2 as game content. Extract it anywhere else and run FollowerForge.exe.
