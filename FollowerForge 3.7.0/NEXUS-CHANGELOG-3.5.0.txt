FollowerForge 3.5.0

- Werewolf now reverts after combat. 3.4.0 (and earlier) cast Skyrim's Beast Form
  visual spell, which waits 10 seconds and turns the follower into a werewolf
  again after revert had already run. That spell is no longer used.
- A fight that ends during the 2-second delay no longer transforms them afterwards.
- Rebuild the follower for the new script to apply. Existing saves of a stuck
  werewolf follower revert on load once the new script is installed.
