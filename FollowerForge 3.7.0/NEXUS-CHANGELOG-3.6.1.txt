FollowerForge 3.6.1

New
- "Copy diagnostics" button on the Review page (also in the Ctrl+K palette). One click puts a
  short plain-text report on your clipboard: which mod manager and profile you use, how many
  plugins were indexed, what you picked for the follower, and what the last build reported.
  If something goes wrong, paste that into your bug report or comment and it can usually be
  answered without a back-and-forth first.
- Your Windows account name is not in it. Home folders are written as %USERPROFILE% and
  %LOCALAPPDATA%, so the report is safe to paste in a public comment.

Fixed
- You can unselect things now. Every optional list has a "Clear" button next to its Browse
  button - all the armor slots, weapons, arrows, belongings, spells, perks, and the single
  picks like face, combat style, legacy outfit and body. Clearing one armor slot leaves the
  others alone. Race, voice, class and location have no Clear because they always need a value.
- The Expert Deck has a "Clear selection" button, and now tells you how selection works:
  click a row to select it, Ctrl+click to add or remove one.
- The deck's tick column was labelled "Selected" and looked clickable, but never was. It is
  now labelled "In cart" - it shows what is in your cart, it is not a button.
  (You could always click a row again to unselect it on the normal lists; nothing said so.)
- Small windows no longer cut off the right-hand side of every page. Below about 1180px wide
  the follower dossier hides itself, but the space it used was still being reserved, so
  headings and buttons were clipped with an empty strip next to them.
- The build button row wraps instead of pushing a button off the edge.

Housekeeping
- Build/CI cleanup and corrected release notes. No change to how followers are built.

Known, and not fixed in this version
- RaceMenu OVERLAYS (tattoos, warpaint, body paint) still do not carry over to a built
  follower. Sculpt, tint and head shape do. A warning for this is the next thing on the list.

Upgrading: unzip over your existing copy, or into a new folder. Your settings, catalogue and
saved followers are untouched.
